# Commercial use and licensing

FOSS Earth original source code is offered under AGPL-3.0-only. Commercial use is allowed under that license. Copyright holders may also offer separately negotiated commercial terms to organizations that need different rights, such as permission to use proprietary derivative code.

Organizations with more than $1,000 USD per month in revenue directly attributable to products or services based on FOSS Earth are invited to contact the project maintainers through the repository. Possible voluntary arrangements include sponsorship, support, commercial licensing, partnership, and case-by-case revenue sharing in a possible 0–10% range. This invitation is not a condition of the AGPL grant.

Third-party packages, map providers, terrain providers, data services, and trademarks keep their own terms. A commercial arrangement does not waive those obligations.
